For grown-ups

He picks a world and taps pictures to hear the words. The green ▶ starts a game: 3 new words, then 6 “find it” questions. Each finished game earns a sticker, and words he misses come back more often.

Greets him and sometimes praises him by name: “Bravissimo, Francesco!” (and “Francisco” in the Portuguese help).

On: short spoken tips in Portuguese (“Esse é o cachorro. Tente de novo!”), and each new word is introduced in Portuguese first. The words themselves are always in Italian.

Make it an app icon: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
No sound? Turn the volume up. If it stays quiet on iPhone, flip the ringer switch on.
